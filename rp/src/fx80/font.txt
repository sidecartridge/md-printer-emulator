; The FX-80 renderer's font: an original 9-pin draft face in the FX-80's grid.
;
; Each glyph is a line "0xNN c" (its code, the character for the reader) and
; nine rows, one per pin, top pin first. A row is eleven positions 1/120 inch
; apart at 10 cpi: '#' fires the pin, '.' does not. The even positions are the
; six main columns, the odd ones the half-dot columns between them. The printer
; cannot fire a pin at two neighbouring positions, and a glyph keeps its last
; two positions blank (the gap to the next character). A glyph uses eight pins:
; the top eight, or the bottom eight for a descender. Capitals and digits stand
; on pin 7, the x-height is five pins, descenders reach pin 9. Lines starting
; with ';' are comments. tools/printer/font.py checks the rules and draws a
; specimen.
